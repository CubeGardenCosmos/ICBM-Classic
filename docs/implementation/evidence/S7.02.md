# S7.02 雷达航迹表与弹着点预测

## Target

雷达按目标 UUID 维护非持久化航迹；每次扫描使用 S7.01 `TrajectoryPredictor` 预测相对雷达高度的弹着点，并按保护半径区分 `THREAT` 与 `DETECTED`。保持 1.12 来袭消息、雷达状态和红石输出语义。

## Operation

- Added `RadarTrack`, `TrackTable`, and `TrackStatus` under `content/abm/radar`, plus the radar-only `Vectors` bridge between Minecraft `Vec3` and pure-Java `Vec3d`.
- Rewired `TileRadarStation.doScan` to update positions, velocities, per-scan acceleration, last-seen tick, predicted impact and impact ticks. It calls `TrajectoryPredictor.predictImpact(position, velocity, acceleration, radarY, ConfigAbm.PREDICTION_MAX_TICKS)`; a predicted horizontal impact distance at or below `triggerRange` sets `THREAT`, otherwise `DETECTED`.
- Missing contacts become `LOST` for one scan and are removed on the next; intercepted tracks have a 40-tick purge window. The incoming and detected missile/entity lists are read-only derived views. The obsolete `isMissileGoingToHit` check is removed; `isThreat(Entity)` remains available and excludes the existing SAM entity type.
- Redstone strength is `min(15, THREAT count + ENGAGED count)`. `ConfigAbm` registers `use_missile_target_data=true` and `prediction_max_ticks=600` in `ConfigManager.CONFIG_CLASSES`.
- Added GameTests for inbound threat classification and predicted impact, sideways non-threat classification, LOST/removal lifecycle, and threat-count redstone output.
